1. Introduction

FarmDirect ("we", "us", "our") connects farmers and buyers directly through our website and mobile app (the "Platform"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using FarmDirect, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our services:

  • Account information: name, phone number, email address, and password when you register.
  • Identity & KYC data: Aadhaar details and other documents for farmer verification and secure payouts.
  • Farm & product data: listings, crop details, pricing, and photos you upload.
  • Transaction data: orders, payment details, bank account information for payouts, and delivery addresses.
  • Location data: approximate or precise location to show nearby products and estimate delivery.
  • Usage data: pages visited, searches, device type, and app interactions, collected automatically.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process orders, payments and payouts
  • Verify farmer identity through KYC
  • Connect buyers with relevant nearby listings
  • Provide customer support and respond to queries
  • Send order updates, offers, and service notifications
  • Improve platform performance and prevent fraud

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Other users: farmers and buyers see limited details (name, product, delivery location) needed to complete an order.
  • Delivery partners: to fulfil pickup and delivery of orders.
  • Payment processors: to handle secure transactions and payouts.
  • Service providers: cloud hosting, analytics, and customer support tools that work on our behalf.
  • Legal authorities: when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember preferences, and understand how the Platform is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not work properly if disabled.

6. Data Security

We use encryption, access controls, and secure servers to protect your information. KYC documents and payment details are stored with additional safeguards. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we encourage you to use a strong password and keep your login details private.

7. Your Rights & Choices

You can:

  • Access and update your profile information anytime from Account & Profile settings
  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data, subject to legal and transactional requirements
  • Opt out of promotional notifications while continuing to receive order-related alerts

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services, comply with legal obligations (such as tax and KYC records), resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

9. Children's Privacy

FarmDirect is intended for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have collected data from a minor, we will take steps to delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the app or website, and the "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the latest revision.
