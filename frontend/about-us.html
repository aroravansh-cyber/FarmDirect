ABOUT FARMDIRECT

Building a Fairer Food Supply Chain

We connect farmers directly with buyers, cutting out middlemen so growers earn more and everyone eats fresher.

Our Story

FarmDirect started in 2021 when our founders visited a village in Maharashtra and saw farmers selling tomatoes for ₹4/kg — the same tomatoes selling for ₹35/kg in the city market just a day later.

We built FarmDirect to close that gap: a simple platform where farmers list their produce directly, and buyers — from households to retailers — order straight from the source. No layers of middlemen, no unfair pricing.

Today, thousands of farmers across India use FarmDirect to sell smarter, and thousands of buyers get fresher produce at honest prices.
