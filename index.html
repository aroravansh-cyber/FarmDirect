Frequently Asked Questions

View All FAQs →
How to list and sell my organic crops?

Go to List & Sell Products, tap “Add product”, fill in crop details, price and photos, then submit for review.

How do payouts and secure bank transfers work?

Payouts are sent to your verified bank account after the buyer confirms delivery.

What is the delivery and logistics process?

Choose pickup or partner delivery when a order comes in. You’ll see tracking updates in Orders.

How can I update my Aadhaar details?

Open KYC & Aadhaar in your profile, upload the new document and wait for verification.

Is there any commission on sales?

Commission details are listed in the Terms & Conditions page under Fees.
